## Fonts

- Headings: Fraunces, semibold.
- Body and controls: Inter, regular and medium.
- Numbers in tables: Inter with tabular figures.

## Colors

| Name | Hex | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#1B1F24` | Body text on light surfaces |
| Paper | `#FAF8F4` | The main light background |
| Night | `#14171B` | The main dark background |
| Moss | `#3F6B4E` | Primary buttons and links |
| Amber | `#C98A1B` | Highlights and "needs attention" |
| Brick | `#B4432F` | Errors and destructive actions only |

## Surfaces

- Light: Paper background, white cards with a 1px border, no heavy shadows.
- Dark: Night background, cards one step lighter (`#1E2228`), text at `#E8E6E1`.
- Both themes follow the system setting, with the same layout.

## Avoid

- Gradients, glassy blur and stock illustrations.
- More than one accent color on a screen.
- Text below 14px, or gray text that fails contrast.

## Tone of words on screen

- Short and plain. Say what happened and what to do next.
- Name things the way the user does ("cards", not "items").
- No exclamation marks, no emojis, no blame in error messages ("We couldn't read that photo. Try one with less glare.").
